NORA VPN is a client and does not include a VPN connection by itself. You need compatible connection data from your provider, access to your own server, or official NORA VPN access purchased through the Telegram bot linked by the application.

Imported subscriptions, profiles and preferences are stored locally under your Windows account. Installation, repair and updates do not replace that data. Uninstall also keeps it by default.

Opening the official purchase link uses Telegram. Subscription refresh contacts the subscription provider. A non-blocking update check reads the public NORA VPN release metadata from GitHub after startup. Connection checks and country detection may contact external network services.
